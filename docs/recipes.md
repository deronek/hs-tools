# Recipe coverage

The app snapshot contains 92 supported recipes from 119 recipes on the wiki. A recipe is supported when its output and every direct ingredient have a market price: a Bazaar product or an Auction House item priced through the Coflnet API. Recipes with direct coin costs or without Hypixel item IDs (pets) stay excluded. This report lists every recipe and the reason each excluded one is unsupported.

The excluded recipes need coin-cost support, Coflnet identity work for pets, or a market for an untradeable item.
